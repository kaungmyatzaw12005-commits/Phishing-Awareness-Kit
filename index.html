Stay Safe From Phishing Attacks

Learn how cybercriminals trick people and how you can protect yourself online.

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cybercrime where attackers pretend to be trusted organizations to steal personal information such as passwords, banking information, credit card numbers, or OTP codes.

Common phishing methods include fake emails, SMS messages, fake websites, and phone calls.

Types of Phishing

Email Phishing

Fake emails pretending to be from trusted companies.

Spear Phishing

Personalized attacks targeting a specific person.

Smishing

Phishing through SMS messages.

Vishing

Scammers call victims pretending to be banks or companies.

Clone Phishing

Copies a legitimate email but replaces the links with malicious ones.

How to Protect Yourself

  • ✔ Check the sender's email address.
  • ✔ Never click suspicious links.
  • ✔ Use strong passwords.
  • ✔ Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA).
  • ✔ Keep your software updated.
  • ✔ Never share passwords or OTPs.
